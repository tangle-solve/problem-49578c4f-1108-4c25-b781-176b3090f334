### CHEM-000557: Practical lean-electrolyte lithium–sulfur lifetime
- **Subfield:** Electrochemical energy-storage chemistry
- **Open:** Combine low electrolyte-to-sulfur ratio, high sulfur loading and limited lithium excess with long full-cell life, including packaging in energy-density accounting.
- **Status:** A decades-old commercialization barrier. A 2025 study reported 493 Wh/kg excluding external packaging at 2.4 μL electrolyte/mg sulfur, with 70% retention after 220 cycles.
- **Type:** engineering
- **Why:** It would convert sulfur’s inexpensive capacity into durable lightweight storage.
- **Key works:** H. Cho et al. (2025), *Interconvertible and rejuvenated Lewis acidic electrolyte additive for lean electrolyte lithium sulfur batteries*, [DOI:10.1038/s41467-025-62169-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-62169-z).
- **Related:** Chemical engineering: transport in concentrated reacting fluids. Reliability engineering: conversion-electrode inventory loss.
